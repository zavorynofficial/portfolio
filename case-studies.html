Social · Concept

Growth Content System

Challenge: Keep social output consistent without treating every post as an isolated design task.

Approach: Build repeatable content pillars, creative rules, publishing rhythm and review loops.

Automation · Concept

Challenge: Reduce repetitive manual handling between incoming enquiries and the right next step.

Approach: Capture, classify, route and notify using clear rules and human review where needed.
