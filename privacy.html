Privacy

A simple privacy approach.

This static portfolio does not operate a visitor database or analytics dashboard by default.

Information

The contact page uses your device email client through a mailto link. The site itself does not store submissions.

Portfolio media

Some portfolio media can be loaded from the public GitHub repository through GitHub's public Contents API. Browser storage may cache catalogue responses locally to improve repeat visits.

Third parties

Google Fonts may be requested by the site. External links are clearly marked where applicable.
