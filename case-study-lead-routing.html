Lead Routing Automation

A sample operating flow for moving incoming enquiries from capture to classification, routing and follow-up.

RoleWorkflow mapping · automation concept
Use caseInbound lead handling
StatusConcept project
GoalReduce repetitive handoffs
01

The challenge

Leads can arrive through forms, social channels and messages, creating repetitive copy-paste work and delayed routing.

02

The approach

Capture the lead once, classify it using explicit rules, route it to the right owner and keep a human review point for edge cases.

03

The solution

A modular workflow concept: capture → validate → classify → route → notify → log → follow up.

04

Outcome

No savings or conversion results are claimed because this is a concept. The intended benefit is a more reliable and less repetitive lead handoff process.
